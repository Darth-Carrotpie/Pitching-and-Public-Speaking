### 1. Fix the delivery pace—not the word count

## One drill for the next run

Do the **three-second pause drill**:

1. Say the revised version aloud, pausing for three full seconds at each period.
2. Do not pause inside a thought. If you lose a word, stay silent rather than filling the gap.
3. Repeat it three times without reading, then record the fourth attempt.
4. For the current material, aim to finish comfortably inside one minute. If you later add verified detail, keep the rehearsed version within **1:48**, including planned pauses, to preserve a live-delivery buffer.

A transcript cannot reveal eye contact, gestures, posture, or vocal variation. Record the next run on video and score each from 1–5: message clarity, engagement, structure, how it felt, and eye contact. Watch once muted for body language and once audio-only for voice.
