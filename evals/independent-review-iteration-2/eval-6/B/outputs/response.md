Today, we’re saying farewell to someone who has been part of the heart of this library for 30 years.

Rasa, over those three decades, you have done far more than help people find books. You made this library feel like a place where everyone belonged. You knew every regular by name—and, somehow, you often seemed to know exactly what they were looking for before they did. Whether someone came in for a favorite author, a quiet corner, or simply a familiar face, they knew they would find a warm welcome from you.

And, of course, there was always the jar of sweets on the desk. It was a small gesture, but it said so much about you: thoughtful, generous, and always ready to make someone’s day a little brighter. I suspect many people stopped by for a book and left with a sweet, a smile, and a good conversation.

Thirty years of service is an extraordinary achievement. Think of all the readers you have encouraged, all the questions you have answered, and all the lives you have touched—often in ways you may never fully know. The library will not feel quite the same without you here.

Rasa, thank you for your dedication, your kindness, your patience, and all the memories. We hope retirement brings you plenty of time for your own reading, new adventures, and perhaps a well-stocked jar of sweets just for you.

Congratulations on your retirement. You will be deeply missed, warmly remembered, and always welcome back.
