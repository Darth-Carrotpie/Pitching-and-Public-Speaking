## Practice plan

1. Read the speech aloud once with a timer. Aim for 1:45–1:48, not a rushed two minutes.
2. Memorize the opening and closing. Deliver the body from the four cue-card ideas.
3. Rehearse three times aloud. On the last run, look down only when moving to the next section.
4. Record one run. Check that the three long pauses feel calm and that the final request for applause is clear.

## Three delivery techniques

1. **Let the pauses work.** Hold the silence after “feel like home,” the sweets image, and the legacy line. These are the emotional anchors.
2. **Use lighthouse eye contact.** Look at Rasa when saying her name, then include the whole room when speaking about what “we” will remember.
3. **Keep movement simple.** Stand still and use one open-hand gesture for the names, the sweets, and the final three-part summary.

## Outsider test

Ask one listener whether the opening image—building a library from welcome, kindness, and care—sounds natural aloud. Simplify it if they stumble when retelling it.

## Questions

No formal Q&A is assumed. If someone unexpectedly asks for another memory, do not improvise details; invite them to share their own memory with Rasa after the applause.

## Day-of checklist

- Keep water and the five cue cards within reach.
- Make sure a clock or timer is visible.
- Run the opening and closing once, slowly.
- After the final line, turn toward Rasa and allow the applause to begin.
