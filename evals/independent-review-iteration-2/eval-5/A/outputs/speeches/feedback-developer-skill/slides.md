# Feedback Is a Developer Skill: slides

Presented only · 6 main slides · 16:9 · dark navy, white and teal · bold system sans-serif · practical developer-tool aesthetic

The speech also works without slides. If the venue setup is unreliable, use a printed QR code and deliver from the cue cards.

## Slide 1: A three-word review can stop the work.

- **Visual:** Oversized code-review comment in a red-outlined box, surrounded by empty dark space.
- **On-slide text:** “This is wrong.”
- **Speaker line:** “Picture this. You open a pull request and see a comment: ‘This is wrong.’”

## Slide 2: Clear feedback replaces guessing with a next step.

- **Visual:** A left-to-right flow with three large connected nodes; highlight the final node.
- **On-slide text:** Risk → Reason → Route
- **Speaker line:** “The receiver gets a risk, a reason and a route forward.”

## Slide 3: Useful feedback is specific and human.

- **Visual:** A compact process diagram, with each label appearing in sequence.
- **On-slide text:** Notice → Effect → Next step
- **Speaker line:** “Say what you noticed. Explain the effect. Invite the next step.”

## Slide 4: Feedback improves when you practise the next sentence.

- **Visual:** Before-and-after transformation. The vague prompt shrinks; the targeted question grows.
- **On-slide text:** “Thoughts?” → “Where could this fail?”
- **Speaker line:** “When you ask for feedback, replace ‘Thoughts?’ with a target.”

## Slide 5: Clear concerns turn reviews into shared debugging.

- **Visual:** Two opposing labels merge into one side facing a highlighted problem.
- **On-slide text:** You vs me ↓ Us vs problem
- **Speaker line:** “Not you versus me, but both of us versus the problem.”

## Slide 6: Make feedback part of your developer toolkit.

- **Visual:** Large workshop date beside a large, high-contrast QR code. Keep the QR clear of other elements.
- **On-slide text:** FREE · 12 NOVEMBER · QR · URL
- **Speaker line:** “Scan the code and save your place for 12 November.”
- **Required replacement:** [NEEDS SOURCE: final signup URL; generate and test its QR code].

## Optional Q&A backup slide specifications

These are not part of the timed talk. Do not advance beyond Slide 6 unless a related question is asked.

### Backup A: Direct feedback can still give a next step.

- **Visual:** A straight process arrow ending at an actionable request.
- **On-slide text:** Concern → Reason → Next step
- **Use for:** “Shouldn’t reviewers just be direct?”

### Backup B: Disagreement still needs a decision path.

- **Visual:** A branching discussion that rejoins at a named decision owner.
- **On-slide text:** Concern → Trade-off → Owner
- **Use for:** “What if we still disagree?”

## Build notes

- Minimum type size: 30 pt; aim for 44 pt or larger.
- Use no decorative photos or stock-code imagery.
- Keep animations to simple reveals on Slides 2–4.
- Test the final QR code from the back of the room on at least two phones.
