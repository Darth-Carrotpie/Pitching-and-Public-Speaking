# End-of-Year Dinner and March Launch: brief

## Assumptions

- Live end-of-year team dinner, without slides or Q&A.
- The speaker leads or represents the team.
- The audience already knows the product and the March launch context.
- The tone should be warm, sincere, and lightly energizing rather than corporate or comedic.
- No achievements or personal stories were supplied, so the speech makes no specific claims about results.
- The two-minute slot is a ceiling. Timing uses 130 words per minute and a 108-second limit, leaving room for natural delivery.

## Goal and message

**Goal — unconfirmed:** After this, the team will celebrate one another tonight and, on returning to work, bring focus and mutual support to the March launch.

**Selected message — unconfirmed:** This team made this year count—and March is ours.

## Arguments

| Tag | Listener value | Proof used in the speech |
|---|---|---|
| Your work | Each person feels seen and appreciated. | Visible and less-visible contributions are named in concrete, familiar terms without claiming specific results. |
| March launch | The coming launch gives the team a shared and exciting destination. | The user supplied the March launch; the speech connects it to the experience being created for the people the product serves. |
| This team | Everyone has agency without being asked for heroics. | The speech names practical actions within each person’s control: clear questions, careful choices, and strong handoffs. |

## Elephant in the room

At a dinner, people may resist having next year’s work turned into another corporate pep talk. The speech parks this by leading with genuine recognition, rejecting heroics, keeping the work message brief, and explicitly inviting everyone to enjoy tonight before returning to the launch.

## Structure

**Chosen structure:** Story/change blended with an occasion toast.

- **0:00–0:19 — Look around:** Recognize the people in the room and state the message.
- **0:19–0:36 — Your work:** Thank them for visible and less-visible contributions.
- **0:36–0:57 — March launch:** Turn the date into a shared destination.
- **0:57–1:20 — This team:** Replace heroics with clear, mutual action and summarize the three ideas.
- **1:20–1:40 — Toast and action:** Raise a glass, enjoy tonight, then return ready for the next decision and handoff.

No slides are needed.
