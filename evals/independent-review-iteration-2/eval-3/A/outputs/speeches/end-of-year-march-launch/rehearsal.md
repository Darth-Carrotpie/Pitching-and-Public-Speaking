## Practice plan

1. Read the speech aloud once. Simplify any line that catches in your mouth.
2. Deliver the opening and closing three times from memory. These carry the emotional weight.
3. Record one full run, aiming for 1:35–1:45. Check that each long pause is silent rather than filled with “um.”
4. Do one final run from the cue cards. Look up for each complete thought, especially during the toast.

## Three techniques

1. **Lighthouse eye contact:** On “look around,” actually look around. During the body, give one full thought to one person or table before moving your gaze.
2. **Pause and contrast:** Let the silence after “well done” and “mattered” do the emotional work. Add energy when the speech turns toward March.
3. **Grounded stillness:** Stand with both feet planted and keep the glass low. Raise it only after saying “Raise your glass,” making the closing feel deliberate.

## Day-of checklist

- Confirm where the speech falls in the dinner and whether a microphone is needed.
- Keep water nearby; save the first alcoholic drink until after speaking.
- Silence the phone and remove anything noisy from pockets.
- Run the first and final lines once.
- Wait until glasses are filled before beginning.
- Keep the closing even if an earlier speaker runs long; use the marked cut instead.
