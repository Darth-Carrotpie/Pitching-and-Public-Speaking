## Elephant in the room

Fourteen pilots are early evidence, not proof that the business will scale. The pitch parks this directly: “Fourteen pilots are a starting point, not the finish line.” It then limits the claim to the signals supplied—less prep waste and paying restaurants.

## Structure considered

1. **Proof: tell → justify → summarize → invite — selected.** The strongest supplied material is traction, and this structure reaches it quickly.
2. **Monroe: attention → problem → solution → visualize → action.** Suitable for fundraising, but the user did not supply enough market or customer-story material for the visualization section.
3. **Story/change: situation → action → result.** Potentially strong once a specific restaurant story is available.
