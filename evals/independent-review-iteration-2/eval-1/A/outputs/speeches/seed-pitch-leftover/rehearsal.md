## Three techniques to focus on

1. **Pause after proof.** Hold the full pause after “thirty-one percent,” “two hundred and ninety euros,” and the message. Do not soften the numbers by rushing into explanation.
2. **Lighthouse eye contact.** Give each complete thought to one investor, then move to another part of the room. Deliver the CTA to a person, not to the slide.
3. **Move with purpose.** Use one standing position for each tag: daily decision, measured proof, paying demand. Return to centre for the €600,000 ask.

No analogies are used, so no analogy outsider test is required. The closing-time tray image should still be tested on an outsider for clarity.

## Q&A preparation

Each model answer is under 30 seconds. Replace source markers with verified facts before the pitch.

1. **What exactly does Leftover do, and how does it work?**
   - **Answer:** “Each morning, Leftover tells restaurant chefs how much food to prepare. I can show the workflow and explain its inputs in diligence. The value is simple: a clear daily prep number.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: product inputs, output format, setup time, integrations, and one screenshot.]

2. **Hardest/numbers question: Could the 31% be measurement error or selection bias?**
   - **Answer:** “Thirty-one percent is our reported prep-waste result. I’ll answer the methodology with the baseline, measurement window, calculation, exclusions, and restaurant-level spread—not with a headline alone. That evidence is what supports our waste claim.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: baseline, measurement window, metric definition, aggregation method, exclusions, and restaurant-level distribution.]

3. **Are the nine paying restaurants part of the fourteen pilots, and are they still active?**
   - **Answer:** “We have fourteen pilot restaurants, and nine restaurants now pay €290 per month. I will confirm the exact cohort overlap and retention rather than infer it. The signal we can state today is nine paying restaurants.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: cohort overlap, start dates, active status, renewals, churn, and payment evidence.]

4. **Why €290 per month, and does the customer earn that back?**
   - **Answer:** “€290 is the current monthly price. The diligence answer needs the contract terms, gross margin, and measured customer payback. The defensible point today is that restaurants are already willing to pay that price.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: price rationale, contract terms, discounts, gross margin, and customer ROI/payback.]

5. **Elephant: Fourteen pilots—isn’t that too early to invest?**
   - **Answer:** “It is early, and we do not present fourteen pilots as proof of scale. We present them as two signals worth testing: lower prep waste and paying demand. The next stage must show that both repeat.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: pipeline, sales cycle, onboarding effort, and evidence from additional restaurants.]

6. **How large is the market, and who already solves this?**
   - **Answer:** “We left a market number and competitive claim out rather than invent them. The diligence version will show a bottom-up market and compare Leftover with the chef’s current process and named tools. The traction gives us a real starting wedge.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: serviceable restaurant count, initial geography and segment, current alternatives, competitors, pricing, and differentiation.]

7. **Why €600,000, and what milestones does it buy?**
   - **Answer:** “We are raising €600,000. Before the pitch, I will tie that amount to a budget, runway, and measurable milestones. The purpose should be clear: convert early evidence into repeatable restaurant adoption.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: use of funds, budget, runway, milestones, round structure, and existing commitments.]

8. **Why is this the team to win?**
   - **Answer:** “The short pitch needs one sentence connecting our team’s relevant experience to this problem. I will keep the biographies for diligence and bridge back to our ability to turn these early signals into repeatable adoption.”
   - **Bring:** [NEEDS SOURCE: founder names, roles, relevant experience, prior work together, and hiring gaps.]
