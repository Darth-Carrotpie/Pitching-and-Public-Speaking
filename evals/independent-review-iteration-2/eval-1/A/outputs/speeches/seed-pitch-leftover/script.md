## Opening (0:00–0:24) [SLIDE 1]

[MEMORIZE] The dining room is empty. / In the kitchen, the day’s decision is being made: how much food should we prepare? // Get that number wrong, and a restaurant runs short or wastes food. / **Leftover gives chefs a clear prep number that cuts waste.** //

## Argument 1: Daily decision (0:24–0:50) [SLIDE 2]

Every morning, chefs match preparation to the service ahead. / Too little can disrupt service. Too much can become prep waste. //

This repeats, item by item, day after day. / [SKIP IF LATE] At closing, that tray carries ingredients and effort to the bin. / A better prep decision means less uncertainty before service begins.

## Argument 2: Measured proof (0:50–1:14) [SLIDE 3]

Now the proof. / Leftover has been piloted in **fourteen restaurants**. The reported result: prep waste is down **thirty-one percent**. //

That is not a forecast. It is an operating result from real kitchens. / It shows that changing one morning decision can create a measurable result by closing time.

## Argument 3: Paying demand (1:14–1:44) [SLIDE 4]

Results matter, but investors also need willingness to pay. / Nine restaurants now pay **two hundred and ninety euros per month**. //

They are not just testing the idea. They are paying to make Leftover part of their preparation routine. / [SKIP IF LATE] That gives us two concrete signals: restaurant operators see enough value to pay, and Leftover has the start of a recurring business.

## The early-stage question and the ask (1:44–2:16) [SLIDE 5]

This is early. / Fourteen pilots are a starting point, not the finish line. / But the signals are concrete: less prep waste, paying restaurants, and a product built around a decision chefs make every morning. //

We are raising **six hundred thousand euros** to grow from this base. / The next step is to show that these results and this willingness to pay repeat across more restaurants.

## Closing (2:16–2:42) [SLIDE 5]

[MEMORIZE] Remember three things: **a daily decision, measured proof, and paying demand**. / Leftover gives chefs a clear prep number that cuts waste. //

If that matches your investment thesis, speak with me after this session. / **Book a diligence meeting for next week, and let’s make every morning’s first number a better one.**
