# Leftover — 3-minute investor pitch

**Delivery date:** Thursday, 22 October 2026  
**Pace:** Calm and conversational, with pauses after the traction figures

## Pitch

Every morning, before the first customer walks in, a restaurant chef has to answer one expensive question: how much food should we prepare today?

Prepare too much, and good ingredients—and margin—end up as waste. Prepare too little, and the kitchen risks running short during service. That decision has to be made before demand is fully known.

Leftover gives chefs a clear answer: how much food to prep that morning.

It turns a recurring judgment call into a practical action before ingredients are committed. We are not trying to replace a chef’s experience. We are helping that experience start with a better number.

The early evidence is strong.

Leftover is being piloted by 14 restaurants. Across those pilots, prep waste is down 31%.

And that result has translated into willingness to pay: nine of the 14 restaurants now pay €290 per month. That is €2,610 in monthly recurring revenue, or a €31,320 annualized subscription run rate.

That is not scale yet—but it is clear validation. Restaurants have tried Leftover, achieved a measurable result, and chosen to pay for it. Nearly two-thirds of our pilot restaurants are already paying customers.

Our business model is simple: a recurring subscription at €290 per restaurant per month. Morning prep is a focused entry point into restaurant operations, and that focus is a strength. The decision happens every day, its result can be measured, and its value is tied directly to reducing waste.

We are now raising €600,000 to take Leftover from early validation to repeatable growth: expanding the paying restaurant base, strengthening the product, and building a scalable commercial operation.

The restaurant industry does not need more software that only reports waste after it happens. Chefs need a useful decision before prep begins. Leftover provides that decision.

Fourteen pilots. Thirty-one percent less prep waste. Nine paying customers. Now we are raising €600,000 to scale what is already working.

Leftover helps restaurants prepare closer to what they will sell—so less food, and less margin, gets thrown away.

Thank you.

## Delivery notes

- Pause after “prep waste is down 31%.” Let the result land.
- Say the revenue figures slowly; do not apologize for the company’s early stage.
- Finish on “thrown away,” pause, and then say “Thank you.”

## Prepare for investor questions

Have concise, factual answers ready for:

- How the 31% reduction was measured, over what period, and against what baseline.
- What happened with the other five pilot restaurants and how long paying customers have stayed.
- What information Leftover uses to produce its recommendation and how it fits into a chef’s morning.
- Your target customer, market size, sales channel, team advantage, and acquisition cost.
- How the €600,000 will be allocated, the runway it provides, and the milestones it should achieve.
