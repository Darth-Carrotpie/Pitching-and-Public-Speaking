# Poland dental scheduling: market slide

Presented + leave-behind · 1 slide · style: confident, clinical, high contrast

## Working brief

- **Assumed audience:** early-stage investors
- **Goal:** make investors believe this focused entry market can lead to a much larger opportunity and agree to continue the conversation.
- **Message:** Poland is our beachhead, not our ceiling.
- **Arguments:** scheduling is a recurring clinic workflow; the beachhead can be measured cleanly; the model can expand after it is proven.
- **Elephant:** “Dental scheduling in one country sounds too narrow.” Park it by presenting focus as the entry strategy and separating the verified market from future expansion.

## Slide X: Poland is our beachhead, not our ceiling

  `Poland → penetration → new geographies`

- **On-slide text:**

  `Poland is our beachhead, not our ceiling`

  `[A] clinics × [B] ACV = [C] SAM`

  `Poland → penetration → new geographies`

- **Speaker line:** “Poland is where we focus, prove we can win, and build the launch point for a larger category.”
- **Leave-behind:** Polish dental clinics are the initial serviceable market. The company can grow by increasing penetration and, after validating the model, entering additional geographies.
