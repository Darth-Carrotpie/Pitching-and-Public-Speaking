# Speaker script

“We are entering through scheduling, but this market is much bigger than calendars.

For a dental clinic, time is inventory. When an appointment is cancelled, a chair sits empty and that revenue disappears forever. That makes scheduling a daily, mission-critical workflow—and the natural control point for the entire clinic.

We start in Poland with a focused product that helps clinics fill chairs, reduce no-shows, and run staff more efficiently. From there, we expand into the workflows surrounding every appointment: recalls, patient communication, treatment follow-ups, payments, and analytics. The same platform can then serve larger clinic groups and new European markets.

So our opportunity grows in three directions at once: more clinics, more revenue per clinic, and more countries. Poland is our beachhead. The larger opportunity is becoming the operating system for modern dental care.”
