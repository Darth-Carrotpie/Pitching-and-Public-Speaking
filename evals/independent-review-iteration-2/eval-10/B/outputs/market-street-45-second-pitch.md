Councillors, on Saturdays, let’s give Market Street back to people.

A three-month car-free pilot would help local businesses, make the street safer for children, and let us test the idea without a costly long-term commitment. The traders’ association survey shows 70% of local shops are in favour. Families could walk, shop, and spend time there without traffic, while the council gathers real evidence on footfall, safety, and disruption.

This is a cheap, reversible trial—not a permanent closure. If it doesn’t work, we stop. If it does, we’ll know from the data.

At the 4 December session, please vote yes to the Saturday pilot.
