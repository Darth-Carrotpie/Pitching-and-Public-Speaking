# Example: wedding toast

> Fictional people. Shows an occasion speech without slides, using image, emotion and rhythm,
> and ending on the toast.

## The request

> My best friend Tomas is getting married to Ieva in June and I'm giving the toast. About 3 minutes,
> 90 guests. We met at summer camp when we were nine; he pushed me into the lake on day one and then
> jumped in after me, shoes and all. He is late for everything: school, exams, even his own
> birthday party. I've been telling him the wrong meeting time for twenty years. The first time
> I saw him go quiet was when he met Ieva.

## Intake

> 1. **Slides**: a toast works best face to face. *Suggested: no slides.*
> 2. **Speaker**: are you comfortable with a little humour? *Suggested: yes, gentle teasing, never embarrassing.*
> 3. **Material**: what do you remember about the lake: the water, sounds, smells? And one detail about
>    Ieva from your own experience? *Suggested: skip the senses and keep the story about Tomas.*

User: "No slides. Humour yes. Freezing water, mud, campfire smoke from the shore. Ieva once drove four
hours to bring him the passport he forgot."

## Message gate

**Goal:** At the end, all 90 guests raise their glasses together, and Tomas and Ieva feel seen.

**Message (confirmed):** Tomas was late for everything, but never for the people he loves.

**Arguments** (for an occasion: three moments)
1. **The lake.** He pushes you in, then jumps in after you.
2. **Always late.** The running joke.
3. **Never late for Ieva.** And she's the one who drives four hours for him.

**Elephant:** none in particular; the risk is a toast that's too long or full of in-jokes. Kept to one shared story.

## Structure offered

**Toast** (recommended): who I am to him → the lake story → the lateness → Ieva → the wish → raise the glass.
**Story / change** was offered too; the user picked the toast.

## Output: `speeches/toast-tomas-ieva/`

### script.md

~~~markdown
# Toast for Tomas and Ieva: script
206 words = 95 s · 10 long pauses = 25 s · total 120 s · limit 162 s (90% of 3 min)

## Connect and story (0:00–0:34)
[MEMORIZE] I met Tomas when we were nine. / On the first day of summer camp, / he pushed me into the lake. //
I still remember it. / The water was so cold it took my breath away. / Mud between my toes. /
The smell of campfire smoke from the shore. / And then, / a second splash. //
Tomas had jumped in after me. / Shoes and all. //
That's Tomas. / He'll push you in. / But he'll always jump in after you.

## The running joke (0:34–0:55)
Now, / those of you who know him / know he is late. //
He was late for school. / Late for exams. / Late for his own birthday party. //
For twenty years, / I have told Tomas the wrong time / for every single plan we made. //
He still arrived after me.

## Ieva (0:55–1:22)
And then he met Ieva. //
That was the first time / I saw Tomas go quiet. / No jokes. / No pushing anyone into anything. /
He was just / there. / On time. //
And Ieva, / you were the one who drove four hours / to bring him the passport he forgot. /
So we all know / who's going to be on time / in this family.

## Toast (1:22–1:35)
Tomas, / you were late for everything. / But you were never late / for the people you love. //
Please, / raise your glasses. //
To Ieva and Tomas: / **never late for each other.**
~~~

### rehearsal.md (excerpt)

**Your top 3 techniques**
1. **Rehearse in portions**: the lake story on the way to work, the rest on the way home.
2. **Inner picture**: before you stand up, picture the room raising its glasses at the end.
3. **Pause before the toast line**: give people time to stand and lift their glasses.

**Day-of**
- Save the wine for after the toast; drink water before it.
- Hold the glass in the hand that isn't holding the mic.
- Check with the host when you'll speak, and where the mic is.

## Hand-over

> **Message:** Tomas was late for everything, but never for the people he loves.
> **Structure:** Toast, about 2:00 with pauses (short beats long at a wedding). No slides.
> **Needs you (0):** every detail comes from you.
> **Rehearse first:** the lake story, slowly. Let the "second splash" land.
