# Review, coach, adapt

## Review (`/podium review`)

Input: an existing script, outline or deck (pasted text, a file, or a .pptx/.pdf
read with whatever tool is available).

1. **Reverse-engineer** the goal, message, arguments and structure. If the goal
   is unclear, ask one question: "What should the audience do afterwards?"
2. **Score** it against `checklist.md`. Unlike create mode, show the scorecard:
   each section pass or fix, one line each.
3. **Top 3 issues**, ranked by their impact on the goal (not by how easy they
   are to fix). For each: what's wrong, what it costs the speaker, the fix.
4. **Rewrite the weakest parts**, usually the opening, the closing and CTA, and
   the worst slide. Show before → after.
5. Offer to produce the full set (create workflow, steps 4–7), keeping what works.

## Coach (`/podium coach`)

Input: a rehearsal transcript (from a phone recording or a meeting tool),
ideally with the duration and the script.

Analyse and report:

- **Pace**: words ÷ minutes against 120–140 wpm, and the total time against the
  slot. If the duration is missing, ask for it.
- **Filler words**: count them and list the top ones, in the speech's language.
  English: um, uh, like, you know, so, basically, actually, kind of. Lithuanian:
  nu, tipo, žinai, ane, kaip ir, iš esmės, reiškia. In other languages, find the equivalents.
- **Hedges and passive voice**: "I think maybe", "it is believed", "we'll try to".
- **Sentence length**: flag runs of long sentences.
- **Structure**: Is the message there in the first 30–60 seconds? Are the
  arguments recognisable? Did the summary and the CTA happen? Did it end on "thank you"?
- **Drift**: important script lines dropped or added, if there's a script.

Output: 3 strengths, then 3 fixes (each with a rewritten line or a drill), then
one drill for the next run (e.g. the 3-second pause drill, `rehearsal.md`).

A transcript can't show eye contact, gestures or voice. Say so, and suggest a
video self-check for those (`rehearsal.md`, "Self-assessment").

## Adapt (`/podium adapt`)

Rework an existing speech. Keep the goal, the message and the CTA unless the
user changes them. Everything else is negotiable.

- **Shorter**: recompute the word budget (130 × minutes × 0.9). Cut arguments
  before you cut the opening or the closing.
  - 60 seconds or less (elevator): hook → problem → solution → one proof → ask. One argument.
  - 2–3 minutes: hook, message, 2–3 arguments with one line of proof each, summary, CTA.
- **Longer**: add proof, stories and examples to the existing arguments. Don't
  go beyond 3 arguments.
- **New audience**: redo intake rows 2 and 5, the elephant, and the "island" for
  each argument. Swap the examples and the level of jargon.
- **New format**: talk ↔ pitch ↔ memo; presented ↔ leave-behind deck
  (`slides.md`); with ↔ without slides (turn the visuals into spoken images).
- **New language**: rewrite for the ear in that language. Recreate the rhythm
  devices, idioms and jokes instead of translating them literally. Re-check the
  word count, since languages differ in length.

Then re-run `checklist.md` and the hand-over.
