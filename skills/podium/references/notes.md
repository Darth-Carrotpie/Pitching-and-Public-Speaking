# Cue cards

Cue cards let the speaker deliver without reading. The default delivery method:

- **Memorize** the opening (first 3–5 sentences) and the closing, word for word.
- **Speak the body from cue cards**: story beats, not sentences.

Use the speaker's preferred method from intake if it differs: fully read (only
when every word must be exact, e.g. legal or diplomatic), fully memorized, or
spontaneous from an outline.

## Format (notes.md)

One card per section. Each card fits on a postcard: 5 lines at most.

~~~markdown
## Card 1: Opening [0:00] [SLIDE 1]
(word for word)
"…"

## Card 2: <argument tag> [0:30] [SLIDE 2–3]
- <keyword>
- <number or name>
- story: <2-word reminder>
→ bridge: "<transition line>"

## Card 5: Closing [2:20] [SLIDE 7]
(word for word)
"…"

## Card 6: After Q&A (final close)
"…"
~~~

- Keywords, numbers and names only. Only the opening and closing cards hold full sentences.
- The time marks show the speaker whether they're running late. Mark the body
  beats that can go if time runs short with `(skip if late)`.
